Free, as is

Activity Tracker is free to use and provided as is, without warranty of any kind. It may change, pause or be retired at any time, for example if too few people use it to justify maintaining it.

Your data, your backups

Everything you track lives only in your browser on your device. Clearing the browser’s data, losing the device or the browser evicting storage can delete it, and there is no copy anywhere else to restore. Keeping backups is up to you: export one regularly from Settings → Backup.

Usage stats and feedback

The site counts its use anonymously, and you can send feedback from the app. What that involves, which services are used and how to turn the stats off is described in the privacy policy.

If you send ideas or suggestions, they may be used to improve the app, freely and without any obligation to you. Please do not send anything confidential.
